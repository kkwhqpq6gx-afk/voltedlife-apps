Connect the Dots Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VoltedLife (operated by Kik Rox LLC) ("VoltedLife," "we," "us") handles information in the Connect the Dots mobile app. A policy covering all VoltedLife apps and this website is at voltedlife.com/privacy-policy.

Summary

  • An account is optional. Without one, your journeys and moments — including any photos and videos you attach and any optional date of birth you add to a journey — are stored only on your device.
  • If you sign in, sign-in is offered with Sign in with Apple. Your email address (or Apple Hide My Email relay address), an account ID, your journeys and moments (including an optional date of birth on a journey), and the photos and videos attached to them are stored with our cloud provider, Supabase, in the United States, so they can sync across your devices.
  • Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, is only accessible to your account, and is never sold or shared.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Deleting your account in the app deletes your account and all of its synced data from our servers.

Using Connect the Dots without an account (the default)

Your journeys, moments, titles, dates, optional dates of birth, ages, feelings, reflections, and attached photos and videos are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. We do not receive any of it, except as described under Print / Save as PDF below. Deleting the app, or clearing its storage, removes it from that device.

Optional account and sync

You can sign in from the Account screen to sync your journeys across your devices. Sign-in is offered with Sign in with Apple. (If we offer email sign-in with a one-time code in the future, it will collect only your email address, for sign-in.)

What Sign in with Apple provides to us. Connect the Dots asks Apple for your email address only. It does not request your name. If you choose Apple's "Hide My Email," we receive a private relay address (ending in @privaterelay.appleid.com) instead of your real email. We also receive a unique, app-specific account identifier from Apple. Apple verifies your identity. We never see your Apple ID password.

What we store when you are signed in:

  • Account information: your email address (or Apple relay address), an account ID, your sign-in provider identifier, and sign-in timestamps.
  • Your journeys: journey names and, if you add one, the optional date of birth of the person the journey is about (used in the app to show each moment's age), plus the times the journey was created, edited or deleted, so your devices can merge changes.
  • Your moments: titles, dates (as exact as a day or as rough as a year), any older typed ages, feelings, descriptions, and the times they were created, edited or deleted.
  • Photos and videos you attach, plus small preview images, in a private storage bucket. Photos are uploaded as a resized copy (up to 2560 pixels on the long side). Videos are uploaded as recorded, up to 50 MB each. Larger videos stay only on the device they were added from.

When you first sign in on a device, journeys already on that device are added to your account. The built-in example journey is never uploaded.

Optional imports. If you choose to import an Apple Journaling Suggestion, or to share a photo, video or text into the app, the content you accept is saved as a moment on your device and is uploaded only if you are signed in. We do not receive suggestions or shared items you do not accept.

How we use it: only to sign you in and to store and sync your content across your devices. We do not use your content for advertising, analytics, profiling or training AI models. We do not access your content except when needed to keep the service running or when the law requires it.

Signing out: you can sign out of a device at any time and choose whether to keep a copy of your journeys on that device. Your account and its synced data stay in place until you delete the account.

Where it is stored and how it is protected

  • Synced data is stored with Supabase (database, private file storage and sign-in), hosted on Amazon Web Services in the United States (us-west-2, Oregon). If you live outside the U.S., your data is transferred to and processed in the U.S.
  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by our providers.
  • Access is restricted to your account. Database row-level security and per-account private file folders mean only your signed-in account can read or change your data. Photos and videos are never public. The app fetches them through short-lived private links.
  • Our providers keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP address, device and app version, and request times) for security and reliability. We do not use them to track you.

Photos, camera and microphone

Connect the Dots asks for photo library access only when you choose to attach something, and it reads only the items you pick. It uses the camera and microphone only when you choose to take a photo or record a video for a moment. Selected items are copied into the app's storage on your device, and they are uploaded only if you are signed in. You can turn these permissions off at any time in your device Settings.

Print / Save as PDF

When you print a journey, the app builds the page on your device and opens your operating system's print sheet. To style the printed page, the device loads two typefaces from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com), which means Google receives a standard web request (including your IP address) at that moment. No journey content is sent to Google or to us. Printing, sharing or saving the PDF afterward is handled by your operating system and the apps you choose.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a moment or journey: it is removed from all your signed-in devices. On our servers its text, dates, optional date of birth and attached files are erased right away. A minimal deletion record (identifiers, timestamps and the moment's feeling label) is kept so your other devices know to remove it, and it is deleted along with your account.
  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Account → Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, all synced journeys and moments, optional dates of birth, and all uploaded photos and videos from our servers. You can choose whether to keep a local copy on your device. Any copies in our providers' encrypted backups expire on their normal schedule (typically within 7 days). Step-by-step instructions are also at apps.voltedlife.com/connect-the-dots-delete-account.
  • By email: write to david@voltedlife.com and ask us to delete your account. If you used Hide My Email, tell us the relay address shown in the app's Account screen. We will verify the request and confirm when deletion is complete, within 30 days (usually much sooner).

Service providers

We do not sell or share personal information. The only companies involved are providers that run services for us or that are part of your device platform:

  • Supabase (hosted on Amazon Web Services, U.S.): account sign-in, database and private file storage for sync, only if you sign in.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and App Store distribution.
  • Google: Google Fonts when you print or save a journey as a PDF (IP address only; no journey content).
  • Expo: the open-source app framework used to build the app. The app does not send your data to Expo.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or of VoltedLife.

Children's privacy

Connect the Dots is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account or sent us personal information, email david@voltedlife.com and we will delete it promptly. You can also delete an account directly from Account → Delete account.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to object to its processing. You can view, edit and delete your content in the app at any time. For any other request, email david@voltedlife.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Security and retention

We keep synced Connect the Dots data until you delete it or your account. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we use encryption and per-account access controls to protect your information.

Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the Effective date above, and we will update the in-app privacy information and App Store disclosures before any change in how data is handled takes effect.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, want to make a privacy or deletion request, or need app support, contact us at david@voltedlife.com.
