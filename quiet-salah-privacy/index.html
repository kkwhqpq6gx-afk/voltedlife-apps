Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Operated by: Kik Rox LLC, doing business as VoltedLife

Contact: david@voltedlife.com

Quiet Salah (“the App”) silences your phone when you arrive at a mosque and lets the sound come back when you leave. This policy explains what data the App handles and how. The official copy of this page is at apps.voltedlife.com/quiet-salah-privacy.

Summary

  • We collect no data. Quiet Salah has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • Your location stays on your phone. The App uses it on your device to watch for nearby mosques. It never sends your location, your visits or your settings to us or to anyone else.
  • Everything is stored only on your device, and deleting the App removes it.

Location

Quiet Salah includes a built-in list of U.S. mosque locations (from OpenStreetMap and Overture Maps). It does not download mosque data or anything else from a server.

With your permission, including “Always” location, the App reads your position on your device, picks the nearby mosques from its built-in list and registers them with your phone as small circles (geofences). When you enter or leave one, your phone tells the App, on your device. Geofencing is performed by your phone’s location services (Apple Core Location on iPhone; Google Play services on Android), which operate under Apple’s and Google’s own privacy policies and your device’s location settings.

What is stored on your device

  • Your settings, setup progress and any mosques you have saved or hidden, in an on-device database.
  • The current “at a mosque” status and that mosque’s name, so the Shortcuts actions on iPhone can read it.

This data never leaves your device through the App. Deleting the App removes it.

Notifications, Do Not Disturb and Shortcuts

  • Notifications (for example “Arrived at …” and “Left …”) are created locally on your device. The App does not use push notifications and does not register your device with any notification server.
  • iPhone: apps can’t change Focus directly. Quiet Salah offers Shortcuts actions (“Enter Mosque”, “Leave Mosque”, “Am I at a Mosque?”) and a Shortcuts automation that you add and switch on yourself, which turns Do Not Disturb on or off when Quiet Salah’s notification arrives. These run on your device.
  • Links in the App (such as the shortcut download link or map credits) open in Shortcuts or your browser only when you tap them.

Third parties

We do not sell or share personal information, and the App contains no third-party analytics or advertising code. The App is built with the open-source Expo framework; it does not send your data to Expo. Apple distributes the App through the App Store under its own policies.

Map data credits

Mosque data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL 1.0). Suggested mosques are derived from Overture Maps Foundation Places data.

Deleting your data

All Quiet Salah data is on your device. Delete the App to remove it. You can turn off location and notification permissions for Quiet Salah at any time in your device Settings.

Children’s privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold any personal data from the App, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. For any question, contact david@voltedlife.com.
