Delete your Connect the Dots account

Accounts in Connect the Dots are optional and are used only to sync your journeys across your devices. You can delete your account and all of its data at any time.

Connect the Dots is operated by Kik Rox LLC, doing business as VoltedLife.

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  • Open Connect the Dots and go to My Journeys → Account (or Account on the opening screen).
  • Tap Delete account and confirm.
  • Choose whether to keep a copy of your journeys on the device you are using; nothing is kept on our servers.

Or ask us by email

If you no longer have the app, email david@voltedlife.com and ask us to delete your account. If you used Apple's Hide My Email, include the relay address shown in the app's Account screen (it ends in @privaterelay.appleid.com). We will confirm and delete it within 30 days (usually much sooner). The same address is for any other questions.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address or Apple relay address, account ID, sign-in records).
  • All synced journeys and moments (names, optional dates of birth, titles, dates, ages, feelings, descriptions).
  • All uploaded photos, videos and preview images in the private media folder.

Deletion from our servers is immediate and permanent. Residual copies in our providers' encrypted backups, if any, expire on their normal schedule (typically within 7 days). Copies stored locally on your own devices are not touched unless you choose to remove them; deleting the app removes them.
