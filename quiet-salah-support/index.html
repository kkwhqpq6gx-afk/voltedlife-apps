Quiet Salah silences your iPhone when you arrive at a mosque and brings the sound back when you leave. Setup takes three steps in the app. If something isn’t working, these answers cover the common cases.

Setting up on iPhone

  • Step 1, permissions: allow notifications and location. For location choose Always and keep Precise Location on; mosque detection doesn’t work without them.
  • Step 2, add the shortcut: tap Get the shortcut, then Add Shortcut. On that same screen, switch the automation On. iPhone always adds shared automations switched off, and only you can turn it on.
  • Step 3, test with the phone locked: tap Start test, lock the phone and don’t tap the alert. Do Not Disturb should turn on by itself and then off again. Quiet Salah checks the result for you.

Common questions

The phone didn’t go silent.

Open the Shortcuts app, go to Automation, find Quiet Salah Auto and make sure it is on and set to Run Immediately (not “Ask Before Running”). Then run the test again from Quiet Salah.

The sound didn’t come back when I left.

Go to Settings › Focus › Do Not Disturb › Apps and add Quiet Salah, so its “Left” alert can get through while Do Not Disturb is on.

Calls still ring during prayer.

Optional: Settings › Focus › Do Not Disturb › People › Allow Calls From: No One, and turn off Repeated Calls.

My mosque isn’t detected.

When you’re there, open Quiet Salah and tap I’m at a new mosque to save it. If the app is reacting to the wrong place, use Wrong mosque? Hide it.

Keyboard clicks still make sound.

Settings › Sounds & Haptics › Keyboard Feedback and turn Sound off.

Does Quiet Salah track me?

No. Your location is used only on your phone, and the app has no account, server or analytics. See the privacy policy.
